How it helps

Less double entry. More control.

Nusuh Books puts invoices, stock, payroll, rooms and a restaurant till on one ledger, so the people who keep your books spend their time checking them, not retyping them.

  • One entry moves the invoice, the VAT and the ledger together
  • Buying and selling move the stock and its value in the ledger
  • Reviewer checks, sign-off, and a month that locks
  • Screens and documents in Arabic and English
  • Switch on modules as the business grows, with no new system
