What you must not do

This policy applies to every Customer and Authorised User of the Service. You must not use the Service, or let anyone else use it, to:

  • break any law of the Kingdom or of the country where you operate, including tax, anti-money-laundering, sanctions and data protection laws;
  • issue false, inflated, duplicated or fictitious invoices, hide income, evade tax or zakat, or submit anything to ZATCA that you know to be untrue;
  • use another company’s data or ZATCA credentials without its written authority;
  • enter data you have no right to hold, or sensitive personal data such as health, religion or criminal records;
  • share logins, or let people who are not Authorised Users sign in;
  • upload malware, probe or test the Service’s security without our written permission, overload it, or bypass its limits, permissions or audit logs;
  • scrape, copy or resell the Service, or use it to build a competing product;
  • send spam or unlawful marketing through it.

What we may do

If we reasonably believe you are breaking this policy we may warn you, restrict or suspend access, remove content, or end the agreement, and where the law requires we may report the matter to the authorities. Where practical we give notice first and explain what to fix.

Reporting abuse or a vulnerability

Report abuse or a suspected vulnerability to {{contact.email}}. We will not take action against good-faith researchers who follow responsible disclosure.
