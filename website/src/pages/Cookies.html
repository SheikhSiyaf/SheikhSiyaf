What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. This policy explains which ones the Nusuh Books website and software use. It sits alongside our Privacy policy.

1. What we use

We set only strictly necessary cookies. We do not run advertising or retargeting cookies.

2. Cookies we set

The website itself sets no analytics or advertising cookies.

3. Third-party content

The website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google and your IP address is sent to it.

Product demonstration screens are provided by our application hosting provider.

4. Your choices

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings; blocking the necessary cookies may stop the software from working. Because we set only strictly necessary cookies, no consent banner is shown. If we add analytics or preference cookies we will ask for your choice first.

5. Changes

If we add a new cookie category we will ask for your choice again.
