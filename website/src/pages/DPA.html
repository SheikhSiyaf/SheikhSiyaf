About this addendum

This addendum forms part of the Terms of service between Nusuh Books (“Processor”) and the Customer (“Controller”). It applies to personal data in Customer Data. For a Firm, the Client Company is the controller and the Firm acts for it; the Firm confirms it may give these instructions.

1. Subject, duration and nature

We process Customer Data to provide the accounting, invoicing, ZATCA e-invoicing, payroll and related modules for as long as the Customer subscribes and during the export and deletion period in the Terms.

2. What data and whose

See Annex A below.

3. Instructions

We process personal data only on the Controller’s documented instructions, which are these terms, the Customer’s use of the Service and its settings, and written support requests. If we think an instruction breaks the law we will say so, and we may stop until it is confirmed. The Controller is responsible for having a lawful basis, for giving any required notices and for obtaining any consent needed to enter the data.

4. Confidentiality

Our staff and contractors who can reach Customer Data are bound by confidentiality duties and receive training on the Saudi PDPL, and access is limited to what their job needs.

5. Security

We apply the measures on the Security measures page and Annex B, and keep them appropriate to the risk. The Controller controls who has access inside its account and must use strong passwords, remove leavers promptly, and give the Administrator role carefully.

6. Sub-processors

The Controller gives general authorisation to the sub-processors on our published list. We bind each of them in writing to protections no less strict than these, and remain responsible for them. We give at least 30 days notice by email or in the Service before adding or replacing one; the Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that time, and if we cannot resolve it, the Controller may end the affected service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.

7. Transfers outside Saudi Arabia

Customer Data is hosted in data centres that may be outside Saudi Arabia. We transfer it outside the Kingdom only as the Controller instructs or as listed on the sub-processors page, and only where the PDPL and the Regulation on transfers allow, with the safeguards and risk assessment they require. The Controller agrees that the transfers to our listed sub-processors are covered by its instructions.

8. Helping with rights and compliance

We give reasonable help, by providing export and correction tools and by responding to written requests, so the Controller can answer requests from individuals within the 30 days the PDPL allows. If an individual contacts us directly we pass the request on and do not answer it ourselves unless told to. We also help with impact assessments and with dealings with SDAIA, at the Controller’s reasonable cost where the work is substantial.

9. Personal data breach

We tell the Controller without undue delay and, where we can, within 48 hours of confirming a breach of Customer Data, and give what we know: what happened, the data and people affected, likely effects, and what we are doing. The Controller decides whether and how to notify SDAIA, which the PDPL requires within 72 hours of becoming aware of a reportable breach, and the affected individuals.

10. Return and deletion

At the end of the subscription we make Customer Data available for export for 60 days, then delete it, including from live systems within 90 days and from backups on their normal cycle, unless the law requires us to keep it. The Controller remains responsible for keeping the invoices and tax records the VAT law requires it to keep, at least 6 years, so it should export them before deletion.

11. Audits

On 30 days notice, not more than once a year unless a breach has happened, we give the Controller the information it reasonably needs to check our compliance, such as our current security summary and any independent report we hold. An on-site audit is allowed only if that is not enough, during working hours and without disrupting service or exposing other customers’ data.

12. Liability and order of precedence

The limits in the Terms of service apply to this addendum. If it conflicts with the Terms on personal data, this addendum prevails.
