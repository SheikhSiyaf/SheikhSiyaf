What the software handles

Everything a manufacturing business runs on.

Each area below is a module you switch on for this company. Nothing is locked.

Inventory

Items, levels and value per store, with reorder points. Buying and selling move the stock.

Warehouse counter

Scan to issue or receive materials on a screen built for standing up.

Fixed assets

A register with depreciation posted for you, and disposals recorded properly.

Projects

Budget against actual job by job, with a warning as the budget tightens.

Purchases and expenses

Supplier bills, purchase orders and expense claims with approvals.

HR and payroll

Payroll runs posted to the ledger, end of service and leave.

Sales and invoicing

Quotes, invoices and credit notes with ZATCA e-invoicing inside each one.

Banking and reconciliation

Match the bank statement to the books line by line, across bank, cash and card accounts.

Accounts and reports

Trial balance, profit and loss and the VAT return, with every total drilling down to the entry.
