Questions, answered.

Is it ready for ZATCA e-invoicing?

Nusuh Books is built for ZATCA Phase 2: invoices in the format ZATCA requires, with QR codes. Each company connects from its own Fatoora portal, and every invoice type is tested before the first live one.

How does the {{trial.days}}-day free trial work?

Create your company and use the full software for {{trial.days}} days, with the modules you switch on. When the trial ends you continue on the core fee and the modules you chose.

Do we have to use every module?

No. Six modules are always on. The other ten switch on per company, and an industry preset chooses a sensible starting set that your Administrator can change at any time.

Can my accountant work in it too?

Yes. Accounting firms get one login across all their clients, with each staff member limited to the clients assigned to them.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. The screens and the documents work in Arabic and English, including right-to-left layouts.

Which countries are supported?

Saudi Arabia first, with the UAE and Oman following.

When is support available?

During office hours, {{support.hours}} (Saudi Arabia time). Call {{contact.phoneDisplay}} and we will raise your request and follow it through, or email {{contact.email}}.

We run on Excel today. How do we move?

Customers, suppliers and opening balances come in from Excel, and the screen walks you through each step before you issue your first invoice.
