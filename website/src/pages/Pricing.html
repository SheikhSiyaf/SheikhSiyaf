All questions→
Is the trial really free?

Yes. The {{trial.days}}-day trial is free, and it is the full software rather than a reduced version.

What does the {{money.implementation}} cover?

The one-time implementation of Nusuh Books for your company, with the six core modules: Sales and invoicing, Purchases and expenses, Banking and reconciliation, Reports and VAT, Review and sign-off, Users, roles and audit.

What is the annual fee?

{{money.annualCore}} per year. Each extra module adds {{money.perModule}} per year as a maintenance fee. Your Administrator switches modules on or off, and an industry preset suggests a starting set.

What is the accounts clean-up and setup?

An optional extra of {{money.cleanup}}, paid once, for fixing your existing accounts and setting up your books. Tell us about your company and we will confirm whether you need it.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer in Saudi riyals to our Saudi bank account. The Payment page lists the steps.

Do the prices include VAT?

No. All prices exclude VAT. VAT is charged in addition on your invoice.

What currency are the prices in?

Saudi riyals (SAR).

I run several companies, or a firm with many clients. Does that change anything?

It can, so it is worth a conversation. A firm uses one login across all its clients. Email us or call and we will talk it through.
