Introduction

This policy explains what personal data Nusuh Books (“Nusuh Books”, “we”, “us”), commercial registration no. {{company.cr}}, Saudi Arabia, collects and why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and your rights under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its Implementing Regulations. It applies to the Nusuh Books website nusuhbooks.com and the Nusuh Books software.

Privacy contact: {{contact.email}}.

1. Two roles, two kinds of data

Website and account data. When you visit our website, request a demo, or create a user account, we decide why and how your data is used. We are the controller of this data.

Customer accounting data. When a company or accounting firm uses the software to keep books, issue invoices or run payroll, that company or firm decides what is entered. We process it only on their instructions, as their processor, under our Data processing addendum. If your data is in someone else’s books, contact that company first.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data (health, religion, criminal records, biometrics) and ask you not to enter it in free-text fields. We do not use your data to make decisions about you by automated means alone, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Who we share it with

Only with those who need it to run the service, under written terms that require them to protect it: hosting and database providers, email and support tools, payment processing, and professional advisers. The current list is on the Security measures and sub-processors page. We disclose data to ZATCA or another authority when a customer instructs us to submit an invoice, or when the law or a valid order requires it. We do not share data for advertising.

4. Where data is kept and transfers outside Saudi Arabia

Our software is hosted by our cloud hosting providers in data centres that may be outside Saudi Arabia. Where personal data is transferred outside Saudi Arabia, we do so only on a basis allowed by the PDPL and its Regulation on transfers, including appropriate safeguards and a risk assessment. The providers involved are listed on the Security measures and sub-processors page.

6. Security

We use technical and organisational measures suited to the risk, including encryption in transit, access limited by role, and logging of administrator actions (see Security measures). If a breach is likely to harm you, we will notify SDAIA within 72 hours of becoming aware of it and tell you without undue delay.

7. Your rights

You may ask to be informed about how your data is used, to see it, to get a copy, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to {{contact.email}}. We reply within 30 days; in complex cases we may extend that by another 30 days and will tell you why. Withdrawing consent does not affect earlier processing. If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA).

8. Marketing

We send marketing messages only with your consent, and every message has a simple way to opt out.

9. Children

Our website and software are for businesses and are not meant for anyone under 18.
