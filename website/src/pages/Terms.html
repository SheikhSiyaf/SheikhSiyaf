About this agreement

This agreement is between Nusuh Books (“Nusuh Books”) and the company or accounting firm that signs up (“Customer”). It is for business use only.

Who we are. Nusuh Books, commercial registration no. {{company.cr}}, VAT no. {{company.vat}}, Saudi Arabia. Email {{contact.email}}, phone {{contact.phoneDisplay}}.

1. Definitions

Service means the Nusuh Books software, its modules, mobile layouts, integrations and support. Administrator is the Customer’s user who manages access and settings. Authorised Users are the people the Customer lets use the Service. Firm means a Customer that is an accounting firm managing the books of other companies (Client Companies) through one login. Customer Data is everything entered into or generated in the Service for the Customer or its Client Companies.

2. Account and authority

The person signing up confirms they may bind the Customer. The Customer keeps login details safe, is responsible for everything done under its accounts, and tells us promptly of any suspected misuse. A Firm confirms it has the Client Company’s written authority to use the Service on its behalf and to give the Firm’s staff access.

3. Free trial, subscription and fees

Every new customer gets a free trial of {{trial.days}} days with the full software. A free trial ends on its stated date and nothing carries forward unless the Customer subscribes. Plans, modules and prices are those on the pricing page at signup: a one-time implementation fee of {{money.implementation}}, which includes six core modules; an optional one-time accounts clean-up and setup fee of {{money.cleanup}}; and an annual fee of {{money.annualCore}}, plus {{money.perModule}} a year for each extra module as a maintenance fee. Fees are in Saudi riyals, exclude VAT, which we add at the rate in force, and are payable in advance by bank transfer (see How to pay) within 14 days of invoice. The annual fee renews each year unless cancelled before renewal by 30 days notice. If an invoice is 30 days overdue we may suspend the Service after 7 days written notice. We may change prices on 60 days notice, effective at the next renewal. Refunds: fees already paid are non-refundable, except where the law requires. You may cancel before renewal under clause 3 and keep access to the end of the period you paid for.

We grant the Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the subscription to use the Service for its internal business and, for a Firm, to serve its Client Companies. The Customer may not copy, resell, sublicense, reverse engineer or build a competing service from the Service, except as the law permits.

The Customer (or its Client Company) owns its Customer Data. It gives us the right to host, process and back up that data only to provide, secure and support the Service, and as set out in the Data processing addendum. The Customer promises it has the right to enter that data, and that doing so does not break the law or anyone’s rights. We may use anonymised, aggregated statistics about use of the Service, which do not identify any person or company, to improve it.

6. Tax and ZATCA responsibilities

The Customer stays the taxpayer and is responsible for its own tax and accounting compliance. In particular the Customer must: enter correct invoice data and review it before issue; complete ZATCA onboarding with its own credentials and one-time codes, which we ask it never to share with anyone else; keep the originals of e-invoices and tax records for the period the law requires, at least 6 years, and be able to produce them in the Kingdom; and decide with its own accountant how tax, zakat and VAT should be treated. The Service is built to support ZATCA Phase 2 e-invoicing (Fatoora). We transmit invoices and receive ZATCA’s responses on the Customer’s instruction, but we do not guarantee that ZATCA will accept every invoice, that its systems will be available, or that its rules will not change; we will make reasonable efforts to update the Service when they do. Unless and until we say in writing that the Service holds an approval from ZATCA, nothing here states that ZATCA has approved, endorsed or certified it.

14. Term and termination

The agreement runs until the subscription ends. Either party may end it for the other’s material breach not fixed within 30 days of notice, or immediately if the other becomes insolvent. The Customer may cancel for convenience before renewal under clause 3. On any end, the Customer should export its data within 60 days using the export tools; we then delete Customer Data within 90 days, except as the law requires us to keep it. Sections that by nature continue (ownership, confidentiality, liability, governing law) survive.

We may update these terms with 30 days notice by email or in the Service. If the Customer objects to a material change, it may cancel before the change takes effect and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.
